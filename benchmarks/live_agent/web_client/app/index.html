Qwen3-Omni — live session

Camera and microphone in, speech out, over /v1/video/chat/stream. Turn-taking runs in this browser

This model has no listen/speak decision of its own — its vocabulary contains no such token, and the server never starts a turn by itself. So this page decides when you have finished: either a silence detector, or the button. That is a real difference from a natively full-duplex model, not a shortcut, and it is stated here so the two are not confused. Space bar works too.

Measured, not a preference dressed as a fact. The speech stage emits a granule of 0.32 s every 0.21 s (codec_chunk_frames: 4), so delivery outruns playback and the earliest possible start is also gap-free. It was not always: at codec_chunk_frames: 25 a granule is 2 s and takes 1.34 s to make, which stalls speech about a second in — that is what the second option is for, since this page cannot see the server's setting. If speech ever stutters one word in, switch it.

Short replies are load-bearing: every audio chunk the speech stage generates is appended to its own accumulated array, so a verbose turn both shortens the session and lengthens the wait before you can speak again.
