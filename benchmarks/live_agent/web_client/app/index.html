Settings

Ask for short replies. Every reply is appended to the speech stage's own ledger, so a long one both shortens the session and lengthens the wait before you can speak again.

This page decides when you have finished speaking, not the model. It has no listen/speak decision of its own — no such token in its vocabulary — and the server never starts a turn by itself. That is a real difference from a natively full-duplex model, not a shortcut, and it is said here so the two are not confused. The space bar works as well as the button.
